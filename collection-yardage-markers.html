Yardage markers

Fairway and tee-side distance markers, commissioned for your course.

The commission

Distance markers that match your course

Distance markers designed to match the material language of your course. Our A-frame fairway markers combine timber construction with clear distance indicators. Available as fairway markers, tee-side markers, or both. Each set is designed as part of a complete course commission.

Our yardage markers are built from locally sourced hardwoods using CNC fabrication and hand-finishing techniques. The A-frame construction provides stability on the fairway while keeping the profile low and unobtrusive. Finishes range from natural timber tones to charred treatments, with engraved distance numbers and club branding available as standard options.

Each set is designed to integrate with the visual language of your property. If you are commissioning tee markers or course signage alongside yardage markers, we develop a unified material palette across all pieces so that every element on the course reads as part of a single, intentional design.

The commission process starts with understanding your course layout, distance system, and branding requirements. We develop marker concepts that address both function and aesthetics, with sets typically covering standard yardage positions and options for custom distances. All markers are finished and inspected in our Vancouver studio before shipping across North America and internationally.
